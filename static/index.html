Trend

Condition Rankings

Each condition or tag is compared with every other trade in view (Layers apply). Cloud conditions are read as with or against the trade, so a put below the cloud counts the same as a call above it. The edge is shrunk toward the book average, so a handful of lucky trades can't top the list. Exit factors are partly hindsight. This describes your journal and doesn't predict anything.

Backtester⛶

Universe Screener⛶

Runs an engine across a sector, basket, or list and logs per-name stats plus the pooled portfolio. Pick OU pipeline in the engine box to gate first: the half-life estimator measures each name's clock across five timeframes, names without one are excluded, and each survivor sims on its assigned OU engine. The gate selects on a bar-level statistic, not on P&L, and the timeframe is arithmetic, not search. Low-sample names (under 20 trades) are flagged, not dropped.

Engine Assignment⛶

Fits one pooled theta line per name across 10m/30m/1h/2h/1D bars and snaps each 'ou' name onto the OU engine whose bar size puts its half-life nearest the centre of the shared 2–12 bar band. Arithmetic, never P/L. Dry run shows the table and the would-be watchlist diff; Apply writes engine overrides onto watchlist entries and pauses OU names that lost their clock. Ripster names and names not on the watchlist are never touched.
